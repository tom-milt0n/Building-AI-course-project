# Building-AI-course-project
My project on completion of the University of Helsinki building AI course

# Project Title

Identifying how global warming may affect crime

## Summary

I am aiming to create a model that will be trained on past crime statistics and weather data and hopefully will be able to model how crime in different areas of the UK will develop as summer temperatures continue to rise each summer with global warming. I think it might be a nightmare trying to nail down what variables to consider because of course so much contributes to levels of crime but I think it could be a fun project.


## Background

The world is warming, and with that (on top of the obvious issues) comes many societal issues, including increased crime. Crime increases due to increased aggression and more people leaving homes and belongings unattended. I haven't seen anyone try to model how this may evolve in the future depending on the rise of global temperature so I think it could be a good model to attempt.

Describe the process of using the solution. In what kind situations is the solution needed (environment, time, etc.)? Who are the users, what kinds of needs should be taken into account?

The solution (if successful) could be used by policymakers to identify areas of the uk where increased temperatures are likely to drive a greater increase in crime, and react to that accordingly. This hopefully could be used whenever policy for funding or strategy of policing is being considered, by those in power. It should be able to predict the number of each class of crime that will occur in an area based on the predicted temperature increase.

## Challenges

This project will likely have a number of limitations, most notably the fact that crime is very difficult to model as there are so many variables that affect the prevalence of crime in a certain area. Therefore I struggle to see it becoming an extremely accurate indicator however that is no reason to not try.

## What next?

This project could be become a fantastic tool to use to combat one of the societal impacts of global warming and maybe could be extremely useful in decreasing the growth of crime in the uk.

To make this project succeed it will need ideas from a range of data analysts to ensure that all variables that could be affecting the model are considered and that it functions as accurately as possible.
