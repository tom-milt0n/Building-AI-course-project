# Building-AI-course-project
My project on completion of the University of Helsinki building AI course

# Project Title

Identifying if (and if so, how) weather affects crime in Derbyshire

## Summary

I am aiming to create a model that will be trained on past crime statistics and weather data and hopefully will be able to model how crime in Derbyshire occurs as the weather changes, with possible links to studying how global warming may affect this.


## Background

Crime is a very difficult statistic to model due to being impacted by such a large range of external variables such as motivation, opportunity and means as well as environmental factors. I want to use a large bank of historical weather and crime data to see if any specific weather patterns may cause an increase in a certain types of crime.

## How is it used?

The solution (if successful) could be used by policymakers to identify weather patterns that are likely to drive a greater increase in crime, and react to that accordingly. This hopefully could be used whenever policy for funding or strategy of policing is being considered, by those in power. Furthermore, it could be used by anyone in Derbyshire who wants to know if they need to put a second padlock on the shed when they go on holiday based on the weather forecast!

## Data sources and AI methods
The data I am using will come from the office for national statistics crime statistics, for example:
https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/crimeandjustice/bulletins/crimeinenglandandwales/yearendingseptember2025

AI techniques used will at least involve a regression model that will need training on a large amount of crime and weather etc data. This will also require optimisation and least error calculation.

## Challenges

This project will likely have a number of limitations, most notably the fact that crime is very difficult to model as there are so many variables that affect the prevalence of crime in a certain area. There will also need to be a massive amount of data processed and I'm not sure I have the harware for that.

## What next?

This project could be become a fantastic tool to use to combat one of the societal impacts of global warming and maybe could be extremely useful in decreasing rates of crime in the uk.

To make this project succeed it will need ideas from a range of data analysts to ensure that all variables that could be affecting the model are considered and that it functions as accurately as possible.
